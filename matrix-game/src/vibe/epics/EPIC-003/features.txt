Overview

This is a guessing game where one player gives a clue and the other players try to guess a secret number.

Each round has:

- A category
- A top scale
- A side scale
- A secret number
- One player holding the iPad
- The other players guessing on their phones

The aim is to choose an example from the category that helps the other players find the secret number.

Setup

1. Every player starts the game on their own phone.
2. Each player chooses the language they want to play in.
3. Every player must choose the same difficulty that was selected on the iPad.
4. The iPad controls the main game.
5. The iPad chooses who takes the first turn.
6. The player whose turn it is should take the iPad.

Taking a turn

If you have the iPad:

1. On your phone, press the button for playing with the iPad.
2. On the iPad, start the turn.
3. The iPad will generate the round.
4. Hide the iPad screen from the other players.
5. Hold the Show Secret Number button.
6. Look at the secret number without showing anyone else.
7. Choose something from the category that fits the secret number position on the matrix.
8. Say your answer out loud.
9. Wait for every other player to lock in their guess on their phone.
10. Reveal the secret number.
11. Ask how many players guessed the exact secret number.
12. Enter that number on your phone so your score can be calculated.

If you are playing on your phone:

1. Press Start Turn.
2. Look at the category and scales for the round.
3. Listen to the answer given by the player with the iPad.
4. Guess the secret number on your phone.
5. Lock in your guess.
6. Do not reveal your guess yet.
7. When the iPad player reveals the secret number, enter the correct secret number on your phone.
8. Your score for the round will be calculated.
9. If you guessed the exact secret number, tell the player with the iPad.

Round example

Example round:

- Category: Animals
- Top scale: Small to Big
- Side scale: Scary to Cute
- Difficulty: Easy
- Secret number: 3

In this example, the secret number is 3.

On the matrix, 3 is at the top-right.

That means the answer should be:

- Big
- Scary

A good answer could be something like:

Tiger

This is because a tiger is a big and scary animal.

Scoring

If you have the iPad:

- You get 3 points for every other player who guesses the exact secret number.

If you are playing on your phone:

- You get 1 point if your guess is in the correct row.
- You get 1 point if your guess is in the correct column.
- You get 3 points if you guess the exact secret number.

Ending the game

When everyone wants to finish, every player presses the End Game button.

The scores are added up.

The player with the highest score wins.
